- **The Inbox counts only what waits on you.** The header and tabs no longer add low-priority notices (the digest, stopped factory items, spot-checks, automation results, ready-for-review PRs) to "N waiting on you" or to Reviews; notices have their own **Notices** tab, grouped under their usual titles. A dismissed item now reads "Dismissed" instead of "Resolved", and the closed section is headed "Recently closed", so only items genuinely resolved say so.
