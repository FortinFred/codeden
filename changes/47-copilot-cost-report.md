- **Copilot cost report.** Copilot CLI sessions now show an estimated cost next to their tokens in the session header, the turn footer and the Usage reports, priced from the Claude and OpenAI list prices of the model Copilot ran. Because Copilot is a subscription, the figure is labelled API-equivalent, so it reads as what the same work would cost on API credits and counts toward your daily and monthly budgets. Auto and Default, and the models whose list price CodeDen doesn't bundle, keep showing their tokens without a cost.
